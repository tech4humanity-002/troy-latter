What I would look at first

1 · Establish the portfolio truth

What is in flight, why it exists, who owns it, what it costs, what depends on what, what is duplicated and what outcome each initiative is meant to create.

2 · Map the operating friction

Follow a customer and a worker through the real process. Find the handoffs, duplicate entry, information gaps, chasing, exceptions and workarounds that technology can remove or reduce.

3 · Protect the revenue engine

Give agents better information, preparation, targeting and customer visibility without pushing complexity onto them or expecting every person to become a technology specialist.

4 · Strengthen the operating engine

Improve the work around sales: property management, maintenance, administration, accounts, marketing, compliance, training and management visibility.

5 · Make AI practical

Start with the friction that matters. Use AI and automation where they augment people, increase coverage, improve response or reduce low-value work, with appropriate governance.

6 · Measure what changed

Define useful measures before delivery: time released, duplication removed, response improved, adoption achieved, customer experience strengthened and value realised.
