Dear Nicci,

I am applying for the Executive Advisor: Technology Transformation & Portfolio Delivery opportunity because I see it as a business transformation role first and a technology role second. That distinction is important to me.

Across more than 20 years in enterprise transformation, technology, AI, organisational change and executive advisory, I have worked with CEOs, CIOs, CTOs, CFOs, boards, government leaders and frontline teams. My career across AWS, Oracle and Unisys has been highly customer-facing: understanding the business, finding the real problem, making the difficult choices, designing the path forward, helping people adopt it and leaving capability behind.

That is the approach I would bring to Highland. The opportunity is not simply to produce another technology roadmap. It is to connect strategy, investment, people, data, systems and delivery so the business can scale without adding unnecessary complexity.

Recent property and regulatory transformation

My recent work with real-estate agents and adjacent professionals including lawyers, conveyancers and accountants has given me a current view of the operational side of property. This includes preparation for AML changes and the work required to turn available software capability into an operating model that people can actually use and govern. The difficult part is not simply installing a product. It is training, governance, assurance, evidence, exception handling, management visibility and changing behaviour so controls work in practice.

I have also worked across identity and verification, passports and credentials, foreign ownership considerations, AI enablement, targeted marketing, customer personalisation, data at scale and automation. The common thread is turning change into something useful for people and measurable for the organisation.

The whole Highland workforce matters

Real estate agents are the visible revenue engine, but the operating engine around them is just as important. Reception, administration, assistants, property managers, leasing, maintenance, accounts, marketing, compliance and leadership all shape the customer experience. I look for the leaks between those functions: duplicate data entry, fragmented information, chasing, poor handoffs, maintenance coordination, compliance administration and capability gaps.

The aim is a quieter, faster, more capable organisation. Give agents and support teams better information, better tools and better timing so they can spend more time with customers and less time working around the systems.

Why my enterprise background is relevant

At AWS and Oracle I worked with senior leaders across regulated and commercial environments including banking, finance, government, law and property. Those environments taught me how to work where technology, people, governance and risk all have to operate together.

I also have earlier property experience through Defence Housing Australia and more recent exposure to property data and market intelligence through work involving realestate.com.au and Resi Index.

I would be comfortable working across Highland's locations, with the part-time nature of the role, and with the executive team to establish the roadmap, portfolio discipline and operating rhythm that the next stage of growth requires.
