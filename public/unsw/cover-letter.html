Cover letter

AI Platforms, Operations & Enterprise Enablement

Dear Selection Panel,

I am applying for the Technology Manager (AI) role because it brings together the areas in which I have built my career: engineering leadership, enterprise AI platforms, technology strategy, operational maturity, governance and organisational capability.

Across Unisys, Amazon Web Services, Oracle, Hewlett Packard Enterprise and Tech 4 Humanity, I have helped complex organisations move from emerging-technology interest and isolated pilots toward secure, governed and supportable enterprise capability.

At Unisys, as APAC CTO and Head of Strategic Foresight, I led regional technology strategy and a solutions engineering capability of more than 15 people across six countries. I shaped AI, cloud and service-transformation roadmaps and directed secure LLM and agentic AI pilots with emphasis on explainability, privacy, cybersecurity and supportability.

At AWS, I advised more than 50 CIOs and senior technology leaders across Australia and ASEAN on cloud modernisation, AI and machine-learning adoption, resilience and enterprise platform strategy. Earlier roles at Oracle and HPE strengthened my public-sector architecture, service management and operational-readiness foundations.

I would bring to UNSW a practical leadership style that connects strategy with execution across product management, enterprise architecture, engineering, cybersecurity, privacy, risk, vendors, executives and operational teams.

As a UNSW alumnus, I understand that the University is both a complex institution and a public trust. The opportunity is not simply to implement AI tools, but to build institutional capability and move safely from experimentation to sustainable enterprise use.

Yours sincerely,
Troy Latter

Troy Latter · Sydney, Australia
