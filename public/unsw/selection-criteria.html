Division of Operations

Selection criteria

Evidence against all nine requirements.

1. Qualification or equivalent experience

Master of Commerce from UNSW, Bachelor of Economics, recognised technical credentials and more than 20 years of enterprise technology leadership.

2. AI/ML and advanced technology team leadership

Regional leadership of 15+ across six countries at Unisys, cross-functional technical leadership at AWS and operational discipline from HPE.

3. Strategy translated into roadmaps

Strategic intent connected to architecture, operating model, investment sequencing, governance, delivery standards and capability uplift.

4. AWS and Azure AI/ML services

Deep AWS experience with practical enterprise knowledge of Azure AI, Azure OpenAI, Copilot, Power Platform and API Management.

5. Operational performance

Service definitions, SLAs/SLOs, observability, incident/problem governance, capacity planning and continual improvement.

6. AI product evaluation

Clarify outcome, test architecture and integration, assess security and Responsible AI, examine ownership and cost, then scale, remediate or stop.

7. Stakeholder leadership

Advised 50+ CIOs and worked across engineering, architecture, product, security, privacy, commercial and operational groups.

8. Communication and reporting

Reporting focused on what changed, what is performing, what is at risk, what decisions are required and what action follows.

9. Health and safety

Recognition and control of physical and psychosocial hazards through realistic sequencing, role clarity, escalation and workload management.
