How to use this pack

This pack is designed to be read in order. The Board CV and Board Profile establish who Troy is and what he brings. The Capability Matrix and Judgement in Practice documents show how he exercises judgement at board level. The Good Things statement and email tie the material to this specific opportunity.

Accuracy note: Formal titles, dates, appointment designations, contact details and specific quantified outcomes should be checked against primary records before submission.
