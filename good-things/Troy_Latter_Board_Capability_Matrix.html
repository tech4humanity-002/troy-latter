Troy Latter — Board Capability & Governance Matrix

Purpose

This matrix is designed for board and executive search use. It distinguishes broad capability from claims that require primary-record verification.

Capabilities

Verification priorities

Verification priorities: exact board and committee roles; formal employment titles and dates; quantified outcomes; financial accountability; and any claims of direct board advice.
These pages are HTML renditions of the PDF board pack. For formal submission, use the PDF documents and verify all titles, dates and claims against primary records.
